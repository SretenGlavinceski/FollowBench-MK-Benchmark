    $$\text{Earnings}_{\text{yesterday}} = \$12 \times \frac{5}{6} = \$10$$

**Step 2: Calculate earnings for today**
*   **Rate:** $\$12$ per hour
*   **Time:** One and a half hour ($1.5$ hours)

    $$\text{Earnings}_{\text{today}} = \$12 \times 1.5 = \$18$$

**Step 3: Calculate total earnings**
*   Add the earnings from both days:

Friedman’s perspective creates a specific framework for ethics: **procedural ethics over outcome ethics.** Under this view, a business is ethical as long as it follows the "rules of the game."
